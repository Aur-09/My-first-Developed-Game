AUR09 is a 2v2 knockout fighter with one core twist: the floor is both your weapon and your danger.

Two former best friends, Lurna and Nofia, lead opposing crews. You control Lurna. Your AI ally Iona fights beside you against Nofia and Kest.

Heavy attacks and the Pulse ability crack the arena floor tile by tile. But we guarantee the remaining floor always stays connected — so no player is ever trapped. That's the design principle: pressure without punishment.

Here's the core loop:
- Move to a safe tile
- Land strikes to build energy
- Dash away from warned tiles before they collapse
- Spend 45 energy on Pulse to break the floor under your enemy

The game ships with two arenas — the industrial Ash Yard and the flooded Floodline — plus exploration scenes in the crew's old home and a burning forest.

We built this in Godot 4, with original systems and original art direction.

The MVP is the collapsing-floor combat loop. The next step is rigged character art — we've already generated the direction.
